Return & Refund Policy

ShareWhatsApp

FJ ZONE by Kazi Auto · Naif 9 Building, Shop 8, 9th Street, Deira, Dubai, UAE · Last updated 26 November 2026

This policy applies to all products bought from FJ ZONE by Kazi Auto on kaziautotrading.com, on WhatsApp or in our shop, for delivery in the United Arab Emirates.

Return window

  • You can return or exchange an item within 7 days of delivery (or of collection from our shop).
  • We accept returns of both faulty and non-faulty items, subject to the conditions below.

Condition of returned items

  • We sell new items only, and returned items must be new, unused and not installed.
  • The item must be in its original packaging with all parts, accessories and manuals.

How to return an item

  1. Message us on WhatsApp at +971 58 241 3312 or email Sales@kaziautotrading.com with your order number and a photo of the item.
  2. Return the item in store at our shop (Naif 9 Building, Shop 8, 9th Street, Naif, Deira, Dubai), or by mail / courier to the same address.
  3. We check the item when it arrives and confirm your refund or exchange.

Return shipping costs

  • The customer pays the cost of sending the item back, unless the item is faulty, damaged or we sent the wrong item.
  • There is no restocking fee.

Refunds

  • After we receive and check the returned item, we refund you by bank transfer (or in cash if you return it at our shop) within 7 days.
  • The original delivery fee is not refunded unless the item was faulty or wrong.

Exchanges

Instead of a refund, you can exchange an eligible item for the same item or a different item. If the new item costs more, you pay the difference; if it costs less, we refund the difference.

Faulty, damaged or wrong items

If an item arrives faulty, damaged or not as ordered, contact us within 7 days of delivery. We will replace it or refund you in full, and we cover the delivery cost both ways.

Items that cannot be returned

  • Items that have been installed or fitted to a vehicle.
  • Custom-made or made-to-order items, such as custom headlights.
  • Electrical and electronic items (such as Android screens, LED lights and wiring) once the packaging has been opened, unless the item is faulty.
  • Items damaged after delivery by wrong installation or misuse.

Contact us

  • WhatsApp / phone: +971 58 241 3312
  • Email: Sales@kaziautotrading.com
  • Shop: Naif 9 Building, Shop 8, 9th Street, Naif, Deira, Dubai, UAE
