About FJ ZONE by Kazi Auto

ShareWhatsApp

FJ ZONE is the Toyota FJ Cruiser accessories brand of Kazi Auto, a car accessories shop in Naif, Deira, Dubai. We focus on one vehicle, the FJ Cruiser, and stock Android screens, lighting, interior and exterior parts and off-road gear for it.

Customers can buy online and order on WhatsApp, or visit our shop to see the parts and have them installed by our team.

What we do

  • Sell FJ Cruiser accessories and parts, delivered across the UAE and shipped worldwide.
  • Install screens, lighting, steering wheels and other upgrades at our shop in Deira.
  • Help customers choose the right part for their FJ year and budget.
