FJ Cruiser Android Screen Buying Guide: 9", 13.3" or 24"?

ShareWhatsApp
By FJ ZONE · Kazi Auto, Deira, Dubai · Updated 2026-11-22

An Android screen is the most popular upgrade for the Toyota FJ Cruiser, and for good reason. It turns a basic factory radio into a modern system with Apple CarPlay, Android Auto, maps, music apps and a camera display. But there are many options, and the cheapest screen is not always the best buy. This guide explains what to look for, so you get a screen that is fast, fits properly and lasts.

1. The 3 screen sizes for the FJ Cruiser

9-inch: fits the factory dash

The 9-inch FJ Cruiser Android stereo (from AED 299) sits in the original radio position with a custom frame, and your AC knobs stay as they are. It is the most affordable option and looks neat and factory-like. Comes in black or silver to match your dash.

12.3 and 13.3-inch: the big-screen upgrade

The 13.3-inch screen with touch climate control (AED 2,850) replaces the radio and the AC panel with one large display, so the whole centre of the dash looks new. There is also a 12.3-inch version (AED 1,999.99). This size is the sweet spot for most FJ owners: a big map, a clear camera view and a modern look.

24-inch: gauges and screen in one

The 24-inch AMG-style screen (AED 15,000) replaces the instrument cluster as well, with a digital speed cluster and the infotainment screen in one ultra-wide panel. It is a full dashboard transformation and the most eye-catching FJ interior we build.

2. RAM, storage and what the specs mean

Android screens are listed with two numbers, like 4+32 or 8+128. The first is RAM, and the second is storage.

SpecWhat it means for you
1–2 GB RAMFine for radio, Bluetooth music and calls. Can feel slow with maps and CarPlay running together.
4 GB RAMOur minimum recommendation for wireless CarPlay and Android Auto. Smooth for everyday use.
6–8 GB RAMFastest option. Best if you use several apps, a 360° camera or a dash cam with the screen.
32–128 GB storageSpace for apps, offline maps and music. 32 GB is enough for most people.

On the 9-inch screen, for example, prices range from AED 299 for the 1+32 version up to AED 999 for the 8+128 version with CarPlay, DSP and a QLED display. You can see every option on the product page.

3. Features worth paying for

  • Wireless CarPlay and Android Auto: your phone connects as soon as you start the car, with no cable.
  • DSP (digital sound processor): lets you tune the sound properly, a clear improvement even with factory speakers.
  • QLED or IPS display: easier to read in strong UAE sunlight than cheaper panels.
  • Camera inputs: add a reversing camera (AED 250) for easy parking. Some customers also add a front camera for off-road use.
  • Steering wheel controls: make sure your buttons keep working. Every screen we fit supports them.

4. Installation

A good install matters as much as the screen. Poor wiring causes most screen problems we see, such as random restarts, noise from the speakers and cameras that don't switch on. At our shop in Deira we fit screens with the correct FJ Cruiser harness, so nothing in the factory wiring is cut, and we test the steering buttons, camera, Bluetooth and CarPlay before you leave.

If you buy from us and live outside Dubai, we ship the screen with the correct harness and frame, and we can help on WhatsApp during installation.

Frequently asked questions

Does an Android screen work with Apple CarPlay?

Yes. The screens we sell for the FJ Cruiser support Apple CarPlay and Android Auto. Higher-spec versions connect wirelessly, so you do not need to plug in your phone.

How much RAM do I need?

For smooth wireless CarPlay and navigation we recommend at least 4 GB RAM. 1–2 GB versions work for radio, Bluetooth and basic apps but feel slower.

Will I lose my steering wheel buttons?

No. The screens we fit connect to the FJ's steering wheel controls, so volume, track and call buttons keep working.

Can I keep my AC controls?

With a 9-inch screen, the factory AC knobs stay as they are. The 13.3-inch screen replaces the radio and the AC panel with one unit and gives you touch climate control on the screen.

How long does installation take?

Most screen installs at our Deira shop take a few hours. Message us on WhatsApp to book a time.
