Best FJ Cruiser Upgrades in the UAE (2026 Guide)

ShareWhatsApp
By FJ ZONE · Kazi Auto, Deira, Dubai · Updated 2026-11-22

The Toyota FJ Cruiser is one of the best-loved 4x4s in the UAE. It is simple, tough and great in the desert. But even the newest FJs left the factory without things we now take for granted, like Apple CarPlay, LED lighting and a reversing camera. The good news is that the FJ is easy to upgrade, and the parts are not expensive.

We fit FJ Cruiser parts every week at our shop in Deira, Dubai. These are the upgrades our customers ask for most, and the ones we think make the biggest difference for UAE driving.

1. Android screen with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto

This is the number-one upgrade we fit. The factory head unit in the FJ is basic, and a modern Android screen gives you wireless CarPlay, Android Auto, Google Maps, Bluetooth calls, YouTube when parked, and a proper display for a reversing camera.

There are three main levels:

  • Budget 9-inch screen: the FJ Cruiser 9-inch Android stereo (from AED 299) fits in the factory dash opening. Choose a version with at least 4 GB RAM if you want CarPlay to run smoothly.
  • Big 12.3 or 13.3-inch screen: the 13.3-inch screen with touch climate control (AED 2,850) replaces the radio and the AC controls with one large display. This is the most popular choice for a factory-looking upgrade.
  • The full showpiece: the 24-inch AMG-style screen with digital speed cluster (AED 15,000) replaces both the gauges and the radio with one ultra-wide display. It is our biggest FJ upgrade.

2. Reversing camera and dash cam

The FJ has thick rear pillars and a spare wheel on the back door, so seeing behind you is hard, especially in tight Dubai parking. A reversing camera is a cheap fix that you will use every day. The number-plate reversing camera (AED 250) hides in the plate light and works with any Android screen.

A dash cam is also worth having on UAE roads. It records what happens in front of and behind the car if there is ever an accident or a dispute. The Wolfbox G900 mirror dash cam (AED 1,800) replaces the rear-view mirror with a 12-inch display, records front and rear, for a clean, factory-style look.

3. Lighting: LEDs, fog lamps and ditch lights

Good lighting matters for desert trips at night, dusty conditions and foggy winter mornings on the highway. It is also the quickest way to make an FJ look modern.

  • Fog lamps: our FJ ZONE TrailBeam dual-colour fog lamps (AED 1,200) switch between white for the trail and amber for dust and fog.
  • Ditch lights: the new Y-Series A-pillar light brackets (price on request) bolt to the factory points with no drilling, so you can mount LED pods to light up the sides of the track.
  • Switches: control all your extra lights from one panel with the 6-gang wireless switch panel (price on request). It sticks on with no drilling and has a built-in strobe mode.
  • Tail lights and small LEDs: smoked LED tail lights (AED 1,300) change the whole look of the rear. For a small budget, an LED interior light kit (AED 149.99) is a quick, cheap improvement.

5. Desert and off-road parts

Many FJs in the UAE spend weekends in the dunes. Fine sand and dust are hard on the engine, so a raised air intake is a smart upgrade. A side snorkel (AED 399) or the full snorkel kit for the 4.0L V6 (AED 450) pulls cleaner air from higher up, away from the sand your tyres throw up.

If you fit bigger tyres, the spare often no longer fits the back door. The spare tyre lift-up bracket (AED 220) moves it up for clearance. A rear tow hitch (AED 680) gives you a solid point for recovery and towing.
